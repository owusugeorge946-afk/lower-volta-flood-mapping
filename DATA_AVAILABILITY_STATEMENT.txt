Data Availability Statement

All satellite and ancillary datasets used in this study are publicly available through Google Earth Engine, including Sentinel-1 GRD, Sentinel-2 Surface Reflectance Harmonised, CHIRPS daily rainfall, ERA5-Land, SRTM elevation, JRC Global Surface Water, ESA WorldCover, and GHSL built-up surface data. The Google Earth Engine and Google Colab/Python scripts used for preprocessing, flood mapping, flood-frequency analysis, threshold and sensitivity analysis, hydro-climatic assessment, exposure analysis, inter-sensor comparison, and figure/table generation are publicly available at https://github.com/owusugeorge946-afk/lower-volta-flood-mapping. Archived diagnostic benchmark and threshold-sensitivity outputs used in the manuscript are included in the repository. Processed raster products and the study-area boundary are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request because of file-size and asset-hosting constraints.
